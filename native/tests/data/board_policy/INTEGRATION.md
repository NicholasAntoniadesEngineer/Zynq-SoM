## Explicit native provenance migration

`floorplan_ledger_migrations()` separately records five retirements:
`placeholder_aspect`, `placeholder_min`, `placeholder_max`, `zone_step`,
and `som_side_band`. These have no native board consumer. The parameterized
`interior_dims` kernel remains intact and no unused defaults were introduced.

Three misleading estimator operands (`via_size`, `via_clearance`,
`stack_thickness`) are replaced by `via_ordinary_cost` and
`via_impedance_cost`. These are the compiled estimator policies actually used,
not a claim about physical emitter parameters. The ordinary provider honors
`FloorplanExperiment::ordinary_via_mm`, including zero; the impedance cost
remains its compiled default. Invalid overrides fail with the solver's own
validation. Emitter geometry/stack overrides are not misrepresented as
estimator inputs.

Default estimator bits stay exactly:
ordinary `0x400199999999999a` (2.2), impedance `0x401e666666666666` (7.6).
Reconstructing the old physical impedance formula produces a different last
bit; tests explicitly prove this counterexample. The two estimator calculations
now record the resolved `via_cost` input, including actual ordinary overrides.

Historical fixtures are unchanged. Test-only
`tests/board_policy_ledger_reference.hpp` applies the narrowly authored
`native_ledger_migration.json` provenance delta for ledger comparisons. It
cannot change geometry, events, counts or non-ledger outputs. All unchanged
ledger rows still compare exactly; the eight old rows disappear, two honest
assumptions appear, and the two via calculations change their input provenance.
