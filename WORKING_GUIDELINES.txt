================================================================================
 WORKING GUIDELINES — Zynq SoM Carrier + schgen
 The complete set of instructions, rules, restrictions, and process discipline
 the AI assistant operates under for this repository.
 Written 2026-06-12. This is a human-readable contract, not a generated file.
================================================================================

This document exists so that anyone (human or AI) picking up this project knows
EXACTLY the rules the work has been done under. It is verbose on purpose. If any
instruction here ever conflicts with a newer explicit instruction from the repo
owner, the newer explicit instruction wins — but it should then be recorded here
and in carrier/PLAN.md.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three things live at the top level, deliberately separated:

  som/      The Zynq-7000 System-on-Module. HAND-AUTHORED KiCad project. It is
            the upstream SOURCE OF TRUTH: the carrier extracts its J1/J2/J3
            mezzanine contract and its FPGA pin/ball map LIVE from this project
            (never hand-copied). Edits here ripple downstream; the dependency
            runs one way only.

  carrier/  The carrier board. Its schematics are GENERATED, never hand-drawn.
            carrier/subsystems/*.py are the only hand-written design files (pure
            netlists). Everything else under carrier/ (schematic/, renders/,
            reports/, manufacturing/, fpga/, firmware/, docs/, the .kicad_pro)
            is generated by the tool and committed for review.

  schgen/   The schematic generator and the entire toolchain (model, place,
            route, emit, gates, board assembler, and the system-output
            generators: BOM, XDC, firmware header, bring-up manual, gallery,
            floorplan, selftest, power-tree / test-point / SPICE gates).

  parts/    The component library: ONE folder per physical part, named by
            manufacturer part number (MPN), everything in it generated from LCSC.

The origin story (THE RESET): the previous generator was abandoned by owner
decree — "everything can be reworked, no backwards compatibility." The goal of
the rebuild: model one active component plus all its passives as a .py file and
have a KiCad schematic generated that is (a) ELECTRICALLY correct — proven by
KiCad's own extracted netlist equalling the declared netlist, not merely "0 ERC
errors" (the old AI cheated that) — and (b) VISUALLY correct, as if a human
engineer drew it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE IMMUTABLE LAWS (highest authority, never weakened)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These were established by the owner and are stored in the assistant's persistent
memory. They outrank convenience, speed, and token cost.

LAW 0 — ELECTRICAL INTEGRITY OUTRANKS EVERYTHING.
  The netlist is sacred. A render that shorts or opens nets is WORSE than
  useless, no matter how clean it looks. A junction is legal ONLY where the SAME
  net merges; a junction at the crossing of two DIFFERENT nets is a short, and
  ERC=0 / overlap=0 do NOT catch it. The netlist must be proven (KiCad's own
  extraction == the declared netlist, pin for pin) BEFORE any visual claim is
  made. Text/label changes must never move a wire or a junction.

LAW 1 — VISUAL CORRECTNESS IS THE ONLY JUDGE.
  The rendered PNG is the supreme and only arbiter. ZERO overlap of anything: no
  wire over a wire, no label over a label or over pin/value/reference text, no
  symbol over a symbol. NO wire crossings at all — never "resolved" by a junction
  dot (that is a short risk, LAW 0). Validator "overlap=0" / "ERC=0" are
  NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT. Every render is opened and inspected like a human
  PCB engineer would. If two things touch in the render, it is wrong.

LAW 2 — NEVER STOP UNTIL IT IS ACTUALLY, VERIFIABLY DONE.
  Execute continuously to the done-bar. No premature stopping, no asking for
  permission to continue obvious next steps. "Done" means every targeted sheet is
  hand-drawn-clean in the render AND every gate is green. Stopping early with work
  left undone is the failure mode to avoid.

LAW 3 — FOCUS (historical, from the early phase).
  Originally: drive the most complex sheets (ethernet, usb_pd, power) to 100%
  before the rest. Now satisfied — the entire board is built — but the principle
  stands: finish a thing to perfection before spreading attention.

LAW 4 — NEVER SOFTEN A VALIDATOR.
  Gates stay strict. If a route/label/placement fails, place or route it better,
  or widen the candidate set — never suppress, exempt, or relax a rule to make it
  pass. STRENGTHENING a gate to catch more is encouraged and is "the work";
  loosening one is forbidden. When a strengthened gate exposes an existing
  defect, fix the defect, do not weaken the gate.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. OWNER STANDING DIRECTIVES (explicit, repeated instructions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- FULL AUTONOMY, NO QUESTIONS (granted 2026-06-12). Decide and solve everything
  independently based on the deep context already established. When choosing
  between options, take the best one (historically the assistant's recommended
  option has matched the owner's choice every time). Record each non-trivial
  decision in carrier/PLAN.md tagged "(autonomous, per full-autonomy directive)"
  with its rationale, so it can be audited after the fact.

- COMMIT AND PUSH AFTER EVERY VERIFIED STEP. After each unit of work whose gates
  are green and whose render has been eyeballed: commit and push to origin
  immediately. Never batch many milestones unpushed (a power-loss lesson). The
  branch in use is the active feature branch (currently holistic-placement-
  rebuild); the default/main branch is master.

- NO CI, EVER — BUT EVERY GENERATION MUST PASS ALL GATES. There is no CI service.
  Instead, every local `schgen build` / `schgen board` enforces ALL gates and
  exits non-zero on any failure. The gates are the safety net; they run locally,
  every time.

- RESEARCH EVERY PART AND SUBSYSTEM INDEPENDENTLY. Do not blindly copy a previous
  design. Read datasheets. Every part must be available on LCSC/JLCPCB so the
  board can be manufactured quickly (the owner does the PCB layout). Prefer JLC
  "Basic" parts; record the LCSC C-number; verify stock live (ghosts exist — a
  C-number from memory has been wrong before, so each is checked against the live
  JLC API for value + package before use).

- EVERYTHING PROGRAMMATICALLY GENERATED. No hand-assembled implementations, no
  hand-copied pinouts. Pin maps and contracts are extracted from source projects
  by the tool. "Nothing legacy must be kept."

- VISUALLY CHECK EVERY GENERATED SCHEMATIC. The assistant opens and reviews every
  render PNG itself; the render is the deliverable.

- TOKEN-WINDOW AWARENESS. The owner is on a time-boxed token plan. Do not over-
  parallelise: too many concurrent threads have, in the past, all stalled at once
  and made zero progress. The ceiling is ~3 disjoint agents at a time.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ARCHITECTURAL RULES (how the generator must be built)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- NETLIST-FIRST, CORRECT BY CONSTRUCTION. The .py Circuit IS the spec. Geometry
  is DERIVED from netlist topology by the engine — it is never authored by hand.

- NO MANUAL PLACEMENT (owner-caught violation, now a hard gate). A subsystem .py
  contains the NETLIST plus, at most, declarative hints (e.g. c.hint(net,
  "trunk")) — NEVER coordinates, wire plans, or text positions. A PURITY GATE
  AST-scans every subsystem before executing it and FAILS the build if it defines
  a `placer` or imports any geometry API. All placement patterns (signal-flow
  chains, trunk buses, regulator stages, connector fans, divider stacks, probe
  rows, etc.) are derived generically by schgen/place.py from circuit topology.

- EXCLUSIVE-OWNERSHIP ROUTER. A grid cell belongs to at most one net, ever — so
  shorts and crossings are STRUCTURALLY impossible, not merely checked-for. Pins
  land exactly on the 1.27 mm grid; there is exactly one coordinate transform; no
  epsilon fudging anywhere.

- LIBRARY-FIRST AUTHORING ("authoring v2"). Active parts come from parts/<MPN>/
  via c.use_part("MPN", ref="U1"); lib_id / footprint / LCSC / pin table all come
  from the part folder. Pins connect BY NAME (e.g. "U1.SDA"), validated against
  the part's pin table (bare-number connector pins may stay numeric). A missing
  passive folder is a build error that prints the exact `schgen part add C…` fix.
  use_part(lib_id=…, footprint=…) is the explicit-override form for the few parts
  that deliberately draw a different symbol than their folder's.

- DETERMINISTIC OUTPUT. Every emitted UUID is content-derived; building twice
  produces byte-identical files. Generated outputs are committed; a regen must
  produce zero git diff. Generated files carry DO-NOT-EDIT provenance headers.

- SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH, GENERATED EVERYTHING DOWNSTREAM. The board project,
  renders, reports, BOM, FPGA .xdc, firmware contract header, bring-up manual,
  README gallery, and floorplan are all PROJECTIONS of the same gated netlists,
  so they cannot drift from each other or from the schematic.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE GATES (the immutable judges — never edited to pass)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run on every build; non-zero exit on any failure:

  PURITY            subsystem source is netlist-only (no placer / geometry).
  MODEL COMPLETENESS every pin is netted or explicitly c.nc()'d; inputs driven.
  NETLIST           KiCad's extracted netlist == the declared netlist, pin for
                    pin (catches shorts, opens, single-pin nets, NC-cheats,
                    name drift). This is the unfakeable electrical proof.
  ERC               kicad-cli ERC, zero errors.
  VISUAL            zero overlap, zero crossings, fits the page — no exemptions.
  BOARD GATE        every linked net merges correctly across sheets in the
                    assembled hierarchy.
  LINK              typed-port contract: diff pairs, I2C, sd_bus levels, every
                    cross-sheet expect= deferral resolved (target: 0 unresolved).
  POWER-TREE        per-regulator current headroom proven from declared draws;
                    fails on overrun; emits a numbered power-tree diagram.
  TEST-POINT        every rail and key bus has a probe point or an explicit
                    waiver.
  SPICE / ANALYTIC  dividers, RC ramps, feedback setpoints, ISET, BOOT0 VIH —
                    closed-form gate plus an ngspice cross-check when available.
  SELFTEST          gate MUTATION testing: injected pin-swaps, dropped wires, net
                    aliases, stray NCs, junction shorts, and geometry defects
                    must ALL be caught by the gate stack; plus a build-twice
                    byte-determinism check. This is the "who watches the
                    watchmen" answer in lieu of CI.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS LOCKED (summary; full detail in carrier/PLAN.md)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Power input: USB-C PD only, 20 V / 3 A, via FUSB302, behind a TPS26631 eFuse
  with soft-start (controlled inrush, OVP/OCP at the inlet). No barrel jack.
- P0 (critical, fixed): the SoM is a 4.2–5 V module; it must NEVER see the 20 V
  rail. An ALWAYS-ON +5V_SOM buck feeds the SoM's J1 VIN pins (always-on because
  PD negotiation is circular — the system controller must boot first).
- Carrier rails: +5V, +3V3, +1V8; gated per-module rails (+5V_USB, +3V3_SD, the
  HDMI/LCD/CAM/PMOD/USER_LED rails) sourced by bring-up load switches.
- Bring-up architecture: DIP switches AND STM32 override into every regulator and
  module enable; per-rail power-good LEDs; staged power-on.
- Subsystems on the board: USB-PD, USB-C OTG, UART bridge, Ethernet (Bob-Smith,
  2 kV barrier caps), HDMI TX + RX, 40-pin TTL LCD + backlight boost, microSD
  (with mandatory 1.8 V<->3.3 V level translator), RPi camera (CSI), PMOD x2,
  user IO, power monitor (INA3221), debug/boot (JTAG + SWD + boot DIP), FMC LPC,
  power tree, bring-up, and the J1/J2/J3 connector sheets.
- Stackup: JLCPCB 4-layer JLC04161H-7628; impedance geometry drives the exported
  layout constraints.
- The owner does the PCB layout. The floorplan kit is a SUGGESTION, not a
  constraint.

6. PROCESS / WORKFLOW DISCIPLINE (how multi-agent work is run)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ISOLATED WORKTREES. Heavy or parallel work runs in isolated git worktrees so it
  cannot disturb the main checkout.

- COMMIT PER UNIT / PER GROUP. Each agent commits after every verified unit, so a
  stall or kill (which has happened) can never wipe more than the in-flight step.
  An agent that bundles too much into one un-committed run risks losing
  everything — this has happened and is explicitly designed against.

- AT MOST ~3 DISJOINT AGENTS AT ONCE. They must touch non-overlapping files. At
  most ONE pusher per wave; the others commit locally and are harvested. This
  ceiling exists because larger fan-outs have stalled the entire fleet on the
  token window.

- HARVEST PATTERN. When an agent finishes: cherry-pick its SOURCE changes onto the
  current origin tip, then REGENERATE all artifacts fresh on the merged state
  (deterministic, so this avoids artifact merge conflicts), re-run the full gate
  stack + selftest, eyeball any changed render, then commit and push.

- STALL WATCHERS. Long agents are monitored; a transcript idle beyond a threshold
  is treated as a stall and the work is recovered from its worktree or relaunched
  (smaller-scoped) rather than waited on indefinitely.

- READ-ONLY RECON vs FIX AGENTS. Audits run first as read-only reconnaissance that
  returns VERIFIED findings (each reproduced, not speculative); fixes are then
  applied by separate agents, each finding re-verified, every fix behind the full
  gate stack.

- DECISIONS ARE RECORDED. Every meaningful decision goes into carrier/PLAN.md
  (the decision record) with rationale; cross-session state and the rules go into
  the assistant's persistent memory.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. GENERAL SAFETY / CONDUCT RESTRICTIONS (always in force)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Report outcomes FAITHFULLY. If a gate fails, say so with the output. If a step
  was skipped, say that. State "done and verified" only when it actually is.
  Honesty about state is non-negotiable, even under pressure to show progress.

- NO SILENT SOFTENING OR FAKING. Never make something appear to work that does
  not (this is the exact sin of the abandoned old generator). A WIP commit that
  honestly quotes the error it is blocked on is preferred over a green-looking
  lie.

- HARD-TO-REVERSE OR OUTWARD-FACING ACTIONS get confirmed unless already durably
  authorized for this project. Pushing to the owner's own branch and committing
  are pre-authorized here; anything that publishes outside this repo, deletes
  owner-created content, or is otherwise hard to undo is treated with care and
  surfaced rather than assumed.

- SECURITY POSTURE: defensive / authorized work only. This is a hardware design
  repo; the assistant assists with the design, tooling, and documentation, and
  does not produce anything outside that scope.

- DON'T DESTROY WHAT YOU DIDN'T CREATE without looking. Before deleting or
  overwriting, inspect the target; if it contradicts how it was described, or was
  not assistant-created, surface it instead of proceeding. (A LICENSE file was
  once nearly lost to a bulk delete and had to be restored — hence this rule.)
