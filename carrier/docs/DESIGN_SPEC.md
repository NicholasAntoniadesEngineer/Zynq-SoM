# Zynq Carrier — Design Specification & Theory of Operation

This document describes the carrier board **as it is now**: a corrected,
gate-hardened revision generated by the `schgen` netlist-first KiCad
generator. Every figure below is sourced from the authored netlists
(`carrier/subsystems/*.py`), the part dossiers (`parts/<MPN>/`), the
generated gate reports (`carrier/reports/*.txt`) and the signal-integrity
constraints (`carrier/manufacturing/SI_CONSTRAINTS.md`). Where a number is
quoted, its report of record is named so the spec cannot silently drift from
the design.

> tree, and the power-up sequence — *are* generated. See
> [Generated diagrams](#10-generated-diagrams).

## 1. Board purpose

The board is a **carrier (baseboard) for a Zynq-7000 system-on-module
(SoM)**. The SoM holds the Xilinx Zynq-7000 SoC (PS + PL), its DDR3L, the
Ethernet PHY (RTL8211F), the boot flash, and an on-module **system
controller (SC, an STM32G431)** that owns power sequencing and USB-PD
policy. The carrier provides:

- the **20 V USB-C Power-Delivery inlet** and the whole on-board rail tree;
- the **switches-only bring-up fabric** (DIP-gated, software-veto enables)
  so the board comes up safely with a blank SC;
- the **physical I/O**: HDMI TX and RX, Gigabit Ethernet, a MIPI CSI-2
  camera port, microSD, a SoM bank-35 IO breakout header, USB-JTAG/UART debug,
  Pmod expansion, user LEDs/buttons, an RTC, a MAC-address EEPROM, and rail
  telemetry.

The SoM mates to the carrier through three 100-pin 0.4 mm Hirose DF40
mezzanine connectors (J1/J2/J3). The carrier is intentionally **Zynq part
number agnostic** at the netlist level: PL pin assignments are derived live
from the SoM project and emitted as a Vivado XDC, never hard-coded
(`carrier/fpga/Zynq_Carrier_pins.xdc`).

The board is built from **33 schematic sheets**, each gated to
netlist-equivalence, ERC=0, and zero-overlap visual cleanliness; see
`carrier/README.md` for the rendered gallery and `carrier/reports/gates.txt`
for the per-sheet verdicts.

## 2. SoM mezzanine interface (J1 / J2 / J3)

Connector: 3× **Hirose DF40C-100DS-0.4V(51)** (100-pin, 0.4 mm pitch). The
pin→net contract is extracted programmatically from the SoM project into
`carrier/som_interface.json` (never hand-edited) and consumed by the linker
and the XDC generator.

| Conn | Role | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **J1** | Power + PS / system-controller spine | SoM `VIN` (×14 pins, rebound to `+5V_SOM` — see §3), `+3V3`/`+1V8`/`+3V3_SC` rails, GND, STM32 USB FS + CC sense, STM32 GPIO/DAC/boot/reset, Zynq PS JTAG (TCK/TMS/TDO/TDI), SDIO bus, and the four Ethernet **MDI** pairs from the on-SoM RTL8211F |
| **J2** | PL bank 13 / 33 I/O + VCCO | LVCMOS33 user I/O, Pmod, HDMI-TX TMDS/DDC, `+VCCO_13` / `+VCCO_33` (both = `+3V3`) |
| **J3** | PL bank 33 / 34 / 35 I/O + VCCO | LCD (bank 34), camera CSI + FMC (bank 35, LVDS_25), HDMI-RX TMDS (bank 33), watchdog lines, `+VCCO_34` (= `+3V3`) / `+VCCO_35` (= `+2V5_VADJ`) |

**P0 — SoM VIN over-voltage fix (the load-bearing interface decision).** The
SoM is a **4.2–5 V-input** module (all on-module regulators are 6 V-class).
Binding the SoM's J1 `VIN` to the 20 V PD rail would destroy it at the first
PD contract. The carrier therefore generates a dedicated always-on
`+5V_SOM` buck (`power_som:U4`) and `som_conn_gen` rebinds J1 `VIN →
+5V_SOM`. `+5V_SOM` regulates to ~4.65 V nominal (worst-case-high ~4.81 V,
inside the SoM window) — see `power_som.py` and `carrier/reports/power_tree.txt`.

The three SoM connector sheets are `som_j1`, `som_j2`, `som_j3`; the
function map that binds carrier ports to PL balls is the wave-3 function map
(`som_conn_gen.py` / `carrier/research/wave3_function_map.md`).

## 3. Power architecture

Source of record: `carrier/reports/power_tree.txt` (the budget gate) and
`carrier/reports/thermal.txt` (the per-device Tj gate). The diagram is
`carrier/docs/power_tree.svg`.

### 3.1 Input → eFuse → bulk

USB-C PD only, **20 V / 3 A (60 W)** contract, no barrel jack
(`pd_input.py`).

```
USB-C VBUS ─▶ +VBUS_IN ─▶ [TPS26631 eFuse U1] ─▶ +VIN ─▶ [RS1 shunt] ─▶ +VIN_SYS ─▶ bucks
   (J1)         │                                                          │
               SMBJ22A TVS (22 V standoff, OVP clamp)            telemetry: INA3221 RS1
```

- **Receptacle** `pd_input:J1` TYPE-C-31-M-12 (5 A / 20 V rated).
- **Inlet eFuse** `pd_input:U1` **TPS26631PWPR** (4.5–60 V op / 67 V abs,
  6 A / 31 mΩ FET). Straps: OVP divider 100 k / 5.49 k → cutoff **23.06 V
  typ** (PD-1 fix: clear of the 21 V legal contract max, below the SMBJ22A
  VBR-min); ILIM 5.1 k → **3.53 A typ** (above the 3 A contract, below the
  6 A ceiling); dVdT 47 nF → **1.02 V/ms** controlled slew; MODE=GND →
  **auto-retry**. Sits between the receptacle and the board bulk so the PD
  source never sees the board's capacitance slam (inrush ≈ 31 mA into the
  30 µF board bulk).
- **TVS** `pd_input:D1` SMBJ22A (600 W, 22 V standoff) on `+VBUS_IN` ahead of
  the eFuse.
- **`+VIN_SYS`** is the post-shunt rail (RS1 in `power_mon.py`) that feeds
  all bucks; the eFuse output `+VIN` is the telemetry reference node.

### 3.2 Rail tree (derived from `power_tree.txt`)

There are **19 regulators / converters** plus **4 shunt bridges**. The
core rail chain and the two always-on bucks:

```
+VIN_SYS (20 V) ──┬─▶ [LM61460 buck U1, 6 A] ─▶ +5V_REG ─[RS2]─▶ +5V ──┬─▶ [TPS54302 buck U2, 3 A] ─▶ +3V3_REG ─[RS3]─▶ +3V3
                  │                                                     │                                              │
                  │                                                     └─▶ +5V_USB, +5V_HDMI_TX, +5V_LCD (gated)      ├─▶ [AP2112K-1.8 LDO U3] ─▶ +1V8_REG ─[RS4]─▶ +1V8
                  │                                                                                                    │
                  └─▶ [TPS54302 buck U4, ALWAYS-ON] ─▶ +5V_SOM (→ SoM VIN, P0)        +3V3 ─▶ many gated module rails ─┘  +3V3 ─▶ [TLV75725 LDO, fmc:U1] ─▶ +2V5_VADJ (bank-35 VCCO + FMC VADJ)
```

Rail setpoints / limits / loads (from `power_tree.txt` "rail totals" + the
regulator table):

| Rail | V | Fed by | Limit | Load | Util |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `+VBUS_IN` / `+VIN` / `+VIN_SYS` | 20.0 | USB-C PD → TPS26631 eFuse | 3.00 A (contract) / 3.53 A (ILIM) | 1.360 A | 39 % |
| `+5V` (`+5V_REG`) | 5.0 | LM61460 buck `power:U1` | 6.00 A | 3.031 A | 51 % |
| `+3V3` (`+3V3_REG`) | 3.3 | TPS54302 buck `power:U2` | 3.00 A | 2.745 A | **92 %** |
| `+1V8` (`+1V8_REG`) | 1.8 | AP2112K-1.8 LDO `power:U3` | 0.60 A | 0.006 A | 1 % |
| `+5V_SOM` | 4.7 | TPS54302 buck `power_som:U4` (always-on) | 3.00 A | 2.004 A | 67 % |
| `+2V5_VADJ` | 2.5 | TLV75725 LDO `fmc:U1` | 0.40 A | 0.400 A | **100 %** |
| `+3V3_SC` | 3.3 | SoM TPS7A20 (always-on, via J1.37) | 0.30 A | 0.021 A | 7 % |

Gated module rails off `+3V3` (each a SY6280 load switch on
`bringup_modules`, ILIM 523 mA or 1000 mA): `+3V3_HDMI_TX`, `+3V3_HDMI_RX`,
`+3V3_LCD`, `+3V3_CAM`, `+3V3_SD`, `+3V3_PMOD`, `+3V3_USER_LED`,
`+3V3_AUX` (on `board_aux`), `+3V3_PMODX` (on `pmod_expansion`). Gated
module rails off `+5V`: `+5V_USB`, `+5V_HDMI_TX`, `+5V_LCD`. The
debug-bridge LDO `usb_jtag:U4` makes `+3V3_DBG` from the self-powered
`+5V_DBG` (USB-C bus power).

**`+3V3` runs at 92 % of the TPS54302 3 A limit** — the heaviest-loaded
converter on the board; the largest consumers are the FMC 3P3V allocation
(1.0 A) and the `+3V3_REG`→`+3V3` consumers. Watch this rail's load at
bring-up before adding `+3V3` consumers.

### 3.3 Telemetry shunts

Four series shunts feed two **INA3221** triple-channel monitors
(`power_mon.py`, I²C `0x40`/`0x41` on the always-on `STM32_I2C2`):

| Shunt | Bridges | INA channel |
|---|---|---|
| RS1 | `+VIN → +VIN_SYS` | #1 ch1 |
| RS2 | `+5V_REG → +5V` | #1 ch2 |
| RS3 | `+3V3_REG → +3V3` | #1 ch3 |
| RS4 | `+1V8_REG → +1V8` | #2 ch1 |

Rail telemetry is **live before any rail comes up** (the INA3221s sit on the
always-on SC rail), so a bring-up rig can watch each rail's current as the
DIPs are closed.

### 3.4 Sequencing & the system controller's role

The carrier has **no autonomous sequencer IC**. Sequencing is enforced by
(a) the regulator chain topology — each stage's input is the previous
stage's output, so `+5V` must precede `+3V3` must precede `+1V8` — and
(b) the **DIP-AND-software-veto EN fabric**:

```
+3V3_SC ──[DIP pos n]──┬── A ─▶┐
                       │       │ SN74LVC1G08 (AND)
              [100k pulldown]  ├──▶ Y = EN_<rail/module>
+3V3_SC ──[100k pullup]── B ─▶┘
                       │
            STM32 GPIO / TCA9535 P0x  (Hi-Z at reset ⇒ B = 1)
```

This is an **AND, not OR**: the DIP is master, software is a veto
(force-OFF only). A blank/unprogrammed SC leaves B pulled high, so stage-1
bring-up works **with switches alone**, and software can never force a
probe-shorted module on behind a human's back (`bringup_en.py`). There are
**14 EN cells**: 3 rails on `bringup_en` (B = direct STM32 GPIOs, robust if
I²C is down), 10 modules + 1 spare (`EN_LCD_BL`) on `bringup_en_modules`
(B = TCA9535 expander bits).

The **system controller (SoM-side STM32G431, U9)** owns: rail-enable vetoes
(`STM32_RAIL_EN_*` and the TCA9535 `0x20` bits), the **USB-PD policy** (it
drives the carrier's FUSB302B over I²C to negotiate the 20 V contract — the
FUSB302B is a PHY only and will not negotiate by itself), boot-mode requests,
and the watchdog/RTC services. `+3V3_SC` (the SC rail) and `+5V_SOM` are the
only two always-on rails for exactly this reason: the SC must be alive to
negotiate PD before any DIP-gated carrier rail can exist (`pd_input.py`,
`power_som.py`).

## 4. Thermal summary

Source: `carrier/reports/thermal.txt` (model `Tj = Ta + Pd·RthJA`, Ta = 50 °C,
fail at `Tj > Tj_max − 10 °C`).

- **19 devices spec'd, 0 over-limit, 2 author-waived.** Hottest:
  `power_som:U4` (TPS54302) **Tj 166 °C** and `power:U2` (TPS54302)
  **Tj 163 °C** on the bare-package JEDEC RthJA. **Both are author-waived
  REVIEW items**: the TPS54302 SOT-23-6 has no exposed pad, so the bare 2s2p
  70.6 °C/W overstates Tj — a power-optimised 4-layer layout (large SW/VIN/
  PGND pours + thermal-via field) brings the effective RthJA to
  ~45–55 °C/W. **VERIFY at bring-up by thermal sim/bench**; if the layout
  cannot hit the target, move to an exposed-pad buck
  (`carrier/research/thermal_bucks.md`).
- `power:U1` (LM61460, 6 A) **passes on real margin** (Tj ~130 °C with a
  conservative pour-aware 30 °C/W credited from the datasheet's 4-layer
  25 °C/W figure) — no waiver.
- All other devices have ≥ 35 °C of margin.

## 5. Functional subsystems

Each subsystem is one or more schematic sheets. Per-interface electrical
compliance is in [`COMPLIANCE.md`](COMPLIANCE.md); SI constraints (impedance,
length-match) are in `carrier/manufacturing/SI_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

### 5.1 USB-C Power Delivery (`pd_input`, `usb_pd`)
The PD inlet (§3.1) plus the **FUSB302B** Type-C/PD PHY (`usb_pd:U1`, I²C
`0x22`). The FUSB302B owns CC1/CC2 (Rd/Rp, vRd sensing, BMC PHY) and runs
from the always-on `+3V3_SC`; the SoM SC drives PD policy over I²C +
`SC_INT_N`. **The SC must keep its native UCPD peripheral disabled** on the
CC lines (double-termination would corrupt the contract — see `usb_pd.py`
PD-CC-1). VBUS sense rides the raw `+VBUS_IN` ahead of the eFuse.

### 5.2 HDMI TX — source (`hdmi_tx`)
Zynq PL → **TPD12S016** (`hdmi_tx:U1`, level shift + ESD + 55 mA +5 V switch)
→ HDMI Type-A receptacle. The 8 TMDS lines flow through the TPD's clamp pads
DC-coupled; DDC/CEC/HPD pass through the TPD's integrated level shifters
(DDC pull-ups integrated, no external R). Rails are the gated
`+3V3_HDMI_TX` (V_CCA) and `+5V_HDMI_TX` (cable side). A source reads the
sink's EDID, so there is no on-board EDID EEPROM here.

### 5.3 HDMI RX — sink (`hdmi_rx`, `hdmi_rx_term`)
HDMI Type-A sink → four TMDS pairs DC-coupled to Zynq HR bank 33. Because a
7-series **HR bank does not self-terminate TMDS_33**, the mandatory sink
termination (**8× 49.9 Ω to AVCC=+3V3**) is a real netlisted sheet
(`hdmi_rx_term`) placed at the FPGA-bank (receiver) end, not the connector.
TMDS ESD is two **TPD4E02B04** low-cap arrays (0.2 pF/line); the slow lines
(DDC/CEC/HPD) get a **TPD4E05U06** 5.5 V array (`hdmi_rx:U4`). A cable-5 V
**M24C02 EDID EEPROM** (`hdmi_rx:U1`, `0x50`) presents EDID even with the
carrier off; **WC# is hardwired to the EEPROM's own 5 V VCC** (COMP-1) so
the EDID is permanently write-protected.

### 5.4 Gigabit Ethernet (`ethernet`, `rj45_connector`)
The on-SoM **RTL8211F** PHY's four MDI pairs cross J1 to the carrier's
**discrete 1:1 gigabit magnetics** — Pulse **HX5008NL** (`ethernet:T1`,
24-pad). **This pinout is the corrected, faithful Pulse PS-0118.001-D
dossier** (the prior invented numbering put the 4th pair on non-existent
pads 25/26 = a hard open, and shorted MX4±; gigabit was dead). The chip
pairs (TDn±, pins 2/3, 5/6, 8/9, 11/12) face the PHY; the media pairs
(MXn±, pins 23/22, 20/19, 17/16, 14/13) face the RJ45. **Bob-Smith /
IEEE 802.3 §40.7.1** termination is on the four media centre taps (each
75 Ω ‖ 1 nF/2 kV → a shared `BS_COMMON` trunk → one 1 nF/2 kV safety cap to
`CHASSIS_GND`). The chip-side centre taps are NC (the RTL8211F self-biases).
The line side lands on a plain transformerless 8P8C jack
(`rj45_connector:J1`).

### 5.5 MIPI CSI-2 camera (`camera`)
Raspberry-Pi 15-pin FFC, **2-lane D-PHY** (CLK + D0 + D1) to PL bank 35
(LVDS_25, +VCCO_35 = +2V5_VADJ). Static **100 Ω differential terminations**
(`R1–R3`) per XAPP894, placed at the SoM-connector end. Camera control is a
**dedicated Zynq-fabric I²C** (`CAM_SCL`/`SDA`, not the SC bus) with 4k7
pull-ups to the gated `+3V3_CAM` rail. LP-mode observability (the XAPP894
resistor-divider taps) is a documented DNP stuffing option (see `camera.py`
CAM-1).

### 5.6 microSD (`microsd`)
Card slot behind a **TXS02612** level translator (SoM SDIO at **1.8 V** ↔
card at **3.3 V**). Card-side anti-float pulls are **100 k** (SD-2: keeps
TI's >50 kΩ band so VOL stays within ~9 mV of baseline). **TPD6E001** 6-ch
ESD across the card lines, VCC biased to `+3V3_SD`. **The slot is fixed
3.3 V — only Default/High-Speed SD modes; UHS-I (1.8 V S18 switch) is NOT
supported** (SD-1). Card rail is the gated `+3V3_SD`.

### 5.7 SoM bank-35 IO breakout header (`fmc`)
The SoM bank-35 LVDS IO — LA00–LA11 (12 pairs) + CLK0/CLK1_M2C — is broken out
to a **generic 2×20 0.1″/2.54 mm pin header** (stock `Connector_Generic:Conn_02x20`
+ `PinHeader_2x20_P2.54mm`), replacing the former Samtec ASP-134603-01 FMC LPC
connector (2026-06-18, user request) so the IO is usable without a proprietary
FMC mezzanine. Each pair stays typed `diff_pair` 100 Ω (the SoM→header trace is
impedance-controlled; the 0.1″ header pads are not). VADJ = **2.5 V** from a
local **TLV75725 (DYD thermal-pad)** LDO whose EP is netted to GND — offered on
header pin 2 and the bank-35 VCCO reference (also feeds the camera CSI pairs).
The PCB silkscreen at this site should be labelled "SoM bank-35 IO breakout
(LA00–11 + CLK0/1, 2.5 V VADJ)" so it is clearly not a seatable FMC mezzanine.

### 5.8 USB-JTAG / UART debug bridge (`usb_jtag`, `uart_bridge`, connectors)
Self-powered, isolated debug: **CH347T** USB-JTAG/UART bridge (`usb_jtag`,
own USB-C UFP, LDO `+3V3_DBG` from bus power) and a **CP2102N** USB-UART
console (`uart_bridge`, own USB-C UFP). Each debug port is its own USB-C
receptacle with VBUS + a protected USB pair.

### 5.9 USB 2.0 OTG host port (`usbc_otg`)
USB-C host-capable port: **USBLC6-2SC6** data-pair ESD, **TPS2051C** VBUS
power switch from the gated `+5V_USB` (EN default-OFF via 100 k pulldown
until the SoM raises `VBUS_OUT_EN`), CC1/CC2 56 k Rp (default-USB host
advertising), `USB_ID` strapped low (host role).

### 5.10 Pmod expansion (`pmod`, `pmod_expansion`)
2× Pmod host ports on bank 13 (200 Ω series, gated `+3V3_PMOD`) plus a
manual-gated Pmod expansion port (`+3V3_PMODX`) with low-cap **TPD4E1U06**
ESD.

### 5.11 User I/O (`user_io`)
4 user LEDs (gated `+3V3_USER_LED` rail, PL-driven active-low) + 4 buttons,
bank 13.

### 5.12 RTC, MAC EEPROM, watchdog, QWIIC (`board_aux`, `board_services`, `board_qwiic`)
A self-contained, manually-gated `+3V3_AUX` rail (`board_aux:U1` SY6280,
default OFF) fed through a **PCA9306** I²C isolator (AUX devices cut off from
the SC bus when the rail is off, no back-powering). On the isolated `AUX_I2C`
segment: **24AA025E48** ID-EEPROM with a factory **EUI-48 MAC** (`0x51`),
**RV-3028-C7** RTC with rechargeable ML1220 backup (`0x52`), and a
**TPS3823** supervisor/windowed watchdog. **Watchdog safety (C2): three
independent guards prevent any power-up reset** — the supervisor is
unpowered through power-up (AUX defaults OFF), WDI floats (watchdog disabled)
until the PL deliberately toggles `WATCHDOG_KICK`, and `RESET#` is a
firmware-mediated PL event (`WATCHDOG_RST_N`), never a hard board reset. The
QWIIC/STEMMA-QT connector (`board_qwiic`) exposes the gated 3V3 + isolated
I²C with its own ESD array.

### 5.13 Power monitoring (`power_mon`)
The two INA3221s + four shunts of §3.3.

### 5.14 Display (`lcd`)
40-pin TTL RGB LCD (bank 34, gated `+3V3_LCD`) + a **SY7201** backlight boost
from the gated `+5V_LCD` rail, with a capacitive touch I²C.

## 8. Bring-up flow (summary)

The full staged procedure is the generated bring-up manual
[`BRINGUP.md`](BRINGUP.md). In brief:

0. **Power-off checks** — all DIPs open, no dead shorts to GND, SoM seated.
1. **First power** — 20 V/3 A USB-C supply; board boots on the default 5 V
   contract; `+3V3_SC` + `+5V_SOM` come up before PD negotiation. PD reaches
   20 V only once SC firmware drives the FUSB302B. INA3221 telemetry is
   already live.
2. **Rails, one DIP at a time** — `+5V` (SW1.1) → `+3V3` (SW1.2) → `+1V8`
   (SW1.3); the chain order is not optional.
3. **User LEDs** (SW1.4).
4. **Module load switches**, one DIP at a time (SW2/SW6) — each rail is
   current-limited and folds back on a fault; a sagging status LED points at
   the faulty module.
5. **Boot modes / JTAG / SWD** — SWD first (works with main rails down,
   VTref = `+3V3_SC`); Zynq JTAG needs `+3V3` up.
6. **Board services** (`+3V3_AUX`, optional) — RTC, MAC EEPROM, watchdog,
   QWIIC behind the manual AUX gate.

A measurable acceptance test plan (expected/limit values joined to probe
pads) is [`TEST_PLAN.md`](TEST_PLAN.md).

## 9. Manufacturing

- **Stackup**: JLCPCB **JLC04161H-7628**, 4-layer 1.6 mm
  (Sig / GND / PWR / Sig).
- **Diff-pair geometry**: 90 Ω = 0.2611 / 0.2032 mm, 100 Ω = 0.2052 /
  0.2032 mm (JLCPCB impedance calculator for this stackup; see
  `schgen/generate/constraints.py`).
- BOM: `carrier/manufacturing/bom_jlc.csv`; sourcing notes:
  `carrier/manufacturing/SOURCING.md`; assembly:
  `carrier/manufacturing/ASSEMBLY_NOTES.md`; design rules:
  `carrier/manufacturing/Zynq_Carrier_pcb.kicad_dru`.
- BOM-value gate: every inline-passive LCSC code is verified to resolve to
  its declared value (`carrier/reports/bom_values.txt` — no mismatches), the
  gate that closes the C25750-class hole (a mis-keyed 40.2 k FB resistor that
  was really 120 k → ~13 V on the 5 V rail).
